# Example input: fintech money transfer

**Artifacts (illustrative, described in text so the example is self-contained):**

- `transfer-amount` (390x844): large numeric amount field, currency selector, a light grey exchange-rate line under the field, a recipient chip, and a Continue button.
- `transfer-review` (390x844): a summary card listing amount and recipient under the heading "Beneficiary details", a full-width Confirm button, a full-width red Cancel button, and under them a small grey line "Fee $4.99 - arrives in 1-2 days".

**Journey:** Enter amount and choose recipient, then check details and confirm.

**Platform:** iOS. **Audience:** adults sending money abroad, mostly occasional users.

**Goals:** increase completed transfers; reduce support contacts about unexpected fees.

**Request:** "Review this flow before we ship."
